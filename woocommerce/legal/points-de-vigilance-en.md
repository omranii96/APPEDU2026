# Legal checkpoints: CHARMED PARIS (jewellery dropshipping)

*Internal document, do not publish. English summary of `points-de-vigilance.md`; the French version prevails. This document helps with compliance. It does not replace advice from a lawyer or an accountant. Legal texts checked on 22/11/2026: check the articles again on Légifrance before going live.*

## 1. You are the seller, not Branvas

- You are **fully liable** to the customer for the whole contract (art. L. 221-15 French Consumer Code).
- **The gap between Branvas and French law is at your cost.** Branvas only covers items reported damaged, defective or wrong **within 10 days** of delivery, and refunds in platform credit. It refuses all change-of-mind returns. You therefore bear:
  - the **14-day withdrawal right** (L. 221-18): full refund, including the initial delivery charge, with the item returned to you;
  - the **2-year legal guarantee of conformity** (L. 217-3; defects presumed to exist at delivery for 24 months, L. 217-7): any defect reported after day 10 is at your cost;
  - parcels lost or "delivered but not received": the risk stays with you until physical possession (L. 216-4).
- Price in returns, return postage and resale of returned stock.
- **Never publish Branvas's policy templates as they are.** You need a **return address in France**: [TO BE COMPLETED].
- Branvas cannot cancel a paid order. Customers can withdraw even before shipment, at your cost.

## 2. Delivery information

- Show **"4–9 business days to France"** and a realistic **maximum** (e.g. 15 business days) before ordering (L. 111-1, L. 216-1). No express delivery and no guaranteed date.
- State clearly that orders ship from **outside the EU** (US or China). Never claim "made in Paris" or "Made in France" (misleading origin, L. 121-2).
- Christmas: last order date for Europe is **4 December 2026**. Nothing is guaranteed after that date.
- **Online withdrawal button** mandatory since **19 June 2026** (Ordinance no. 2026-2, art. L. 221-21). Rename the order button to **"Commander avec obligation de paiement"** (L. 221-14). Make the telephone field mandatory.
- **Packaging**: one jewellery box per order, other pieces in sealed bags, no gift message, two orders = two parcels. Never promise "a box per set", a gift message or combined shipping (misleading advertising, L. 121-2).

## 3. Product safety

- Under the **GPSR** (Regulation (EU) 2023/988), you are the **EU responsible economic operator** and, as a private-label seller, the deemed **manufacturer** (art. 13). Product pages and packaging must show your details, the product ID and warnings in French (art. 9 and 19).
- **REACH Annex XVII**: nickel release ≤ 0.5 µg/cm²/week; cadmium < 0.01%; lead < 0.05%. Branvas tests per SKU on request ($300–800, ~15 business days): obtain reports for the 30 selected SKUs, starting with brass and zinc pieces.
- **No "nickel-free", "lead-free", "hypoallergenic", "certified", "tested" or "hallmarked" claims** without a report for the specific SKU.

## 4. Names and descriptions

- **Gold**: below 3 µm, or with uncertified thickness, only "doré" is allowed. Never use "plaqué or" (≥ 3 µm) or "vermeil" (≥ 5 µm). Use "doré à l'or fin" only for 24k plating; otherwise state the carat. In English, avoid "gold-plated".
- **Silver 925**: precious-metal hallmarking now sits in the **Commercial Code, art. L. 831-1 to L. 835-6** (since 1 July 2025). The French hallmark applies to imports, with an exemption for pieces under 30 g of silver. A "925" stamp is not a French hallmark. [TO BE CHECKED with the customs hallmarking office.]
- **Stones** (Decree no. 2002-65): use "moissanite synthétique", "oxyde de zirconium" (not "zircon"), "perles de culture d'eau douce" (never "fines" or "naturelles").
- Never write "waterproof": always "keep dry".

## 5. Prices and promotions

- Prices include VAT, and delivery charges are shown before payment.
- A **struck-through price** must be the lowest price you charged in the previous 30 days (L. 112-1-1):
  - no strike-through at launch or for Black Friday 2026 without that 30-day history;
  - never strike through Branvas's suggested USD retail price.
- No fake urgency. Reviews only from real customers, with information on how they are checked (L. 111-7-2).

## 6. Influencer marketing (Law no. 2023-451, amended 2024)

- "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale" label on every sponsored post. Free products count as payment.
- Written contract above the threshold set by decree [TO BE CHECKED].
- The advertiser (you) is jointly liable with the influencer.

## 7. Invoices, VAT, customs

- Issue a compliant invoice for every order.
- E-invoicing: reception mandatory since 1 September 2026. Issuance and B2C e-reporting from **1 September 2027** for SMEs and micro-businesses.
- **Sales VAT** is yours to collect and file. [TO BE CHECKED with an accountant]: importer of record, import VAT paid by Branvas, the €10,000 EU distance-selling threshold (OSS), and **UK VAT registration from the first sale** if the UK is opened.
- **Low-value parcels**: €3 EU flat duty since 1 July 2026, and the French €2 handling fee since 1 November 2026. **[TO BE CHECKED]**: get Branvas's written confirmation that its DDP covers both.

## 8. Personal data

- Data transfers to **Branvas (US)**: check Data Privacy Framework certification, or sign SCCs before launch [TO BE CHECKED].
- Keep a record of processing activities.
- Cookie banner with a "Reject all" button, and no pixel before consent.

## 9. Other points

- **Legal status**: "EI" mention for a sole trader [TO BE COMPLETED].
- **Trade mark**: search and file CHARMED PARIS (classes 14 and 35).
- **Branvas visuals**: only usable while the subscription is active.
- **Language**: documents in French (Law no. 94-665).
- **Accessibility**: European Accessibility Act, with an exemption for micro-enterprises.
- **Consumer mediator**: join one before the first sale (L. 612-1). The EU ODR platform link is no longer required (closed 20 July 2025).
- **United Kingdom**:
  - Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013: 14-day cancellation right;
  - Consumer Rights Act 2015: 30-day right to reject, claims up to 6 years;
  - Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024: fake reviews and drip pricing banned;
  - UK GDPR;
  - UK product safety rules (GPSR applies in Northern Ireland only);
  - under the ADR Regulations 2015, you must name a certified ADR body when a complaint cannot be resolved, and say whether you will use it.
  
  Do not open the UK until VAT and product compliance are sorted.
