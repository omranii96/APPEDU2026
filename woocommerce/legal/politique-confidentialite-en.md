## 4. Transfers outside the European Union

Our parcels are prepared and shipped from abroad by our fulfilment partner, based in the **United States**, so the data needed for delivery is transferred to it. This transfer is covered:

- either by the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795), if the recipient is certified (art. 45 GDPR). The EU General Court upheld its validity on 3 September 2025 (case T-553/23, Latombe), and an appeal is pending;
- or by the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** (art. 46 GDPR).

## 5. Your rights

You have the rights of **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **portability** and **objection** (art. 15 to 21 GDPR), including the right to object to marketing at any time without giving a reason. You can **withdraw your consent** at any time: use the unsubscribe link in every e-mail, or the "Manage cookies" link in the footer. You may also give instructions on what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise your rights, write to **[TO BE COMPLETED: e-mail]**. We reply within one month. We may ask for additional information if we have reasonable doubts about your identity.

You may lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** (www.cnil.fr), the French data protection authority, or with the authority of your country of residence. [TO BE DECIDED: for the UK, the ICO, ico.org.uk.]

The Site uses HTTPS. Admin access is protected by strong authentication. We apply WordPress and plugin security updates and make regular backups. We store no card numbers. If a data breach presents a risk, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours and, where required, inform you.
