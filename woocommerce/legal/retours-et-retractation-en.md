## 1. Changing your mind: the right of withdrawal

### The period

You have **14 days from receiving your parcel** to withdraw (art. L. 221-18 of the French Consumer Code). You can also withdraw before delivery, as soon as you have ordered. If the period ends on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, it is extended to the next working day.

### Step 1: tell us

Choose whichever is easiest for you:

- the **"[TO BE CHECKED: Withdraw from contract here]"** button, at the bottom of every page, in your account or in your confirmation e-mail. You immediately receive an acknowledgement by e-mail;
- an **e-mail** to [TO BE COMPLETED], with your order number and a clear statement that you are withdrawing;
- the **withdrawal form** below, by e-mail or by post.

### Step 2: send your set back

You have **14 days** after telling us to send your set to this address, **and this address only**:

> **CHARMED PARIS – Returns**
> [TO BE COMPLETED: name or company name]
> [TO BE COMPLETED: return address in France]
> [TO BE COMPLETED: postcode, city]
> France

⚠️ **Never send your parcel to the sender's address printed on the packaging**: it would go abroad, and we could not trace it or refund you.

For a smooth return:

1. Include **every piece of the set**, in its original box or bags if possible, with the accessories you received (pouch, polishing cloth).
2. Add a note with your **order number**, or the form below.
3. Use a **tracked service** and keep the proof of postage.

**Return costs**: [TO BE DECIDED: (A) "They are paid by you. Allow about [TO BE COMPLETED] € for a tracked parcel from France." or (B) "They are on us: we e-mail you a prepaid label."]

### Step 3: we refund you

We refund **everything you paid, including the initial delivery charge**, within **14 days** of your withdrawal at the latest. We may wait until we receive the parcel or your proof of postage. The refund goes to your original payment method, free of charge. In practice, we refund as soon as the parcel arrives, within [TO BE COMPLETED: 3] business days.

If you return only part of an order that benefited from an offer (e.g. "2nd set −15%"), we refund the price actually paid for the returned item.

### Try it on, but don't wear it

You may try your set on as you would in a shop. If it comes back worn, damaged, scratched, exposed to water or perfume, or incomplete, we may deduct the loss of value from the refund. We will tell you, with photos.

## 2. Damaged, faulty or wrong item: the guarantee

A piece arrived damaged, a clasp broke, the gold finish wore off unusually fast, or you received the wrong item? **That is our legal guarantee of conformity, for 2 years** from delivery, plus the guarantee against hidden defects.

1. **Take clear photos** of the defect and, if possible, of the parcel.
2. **E-mail us** at [TO BE COMPLETED] with your order number and the photos. Please report a damaged parcel as early as you can: it is faster for everyone, but your right lasts 2 years.
3. **We reply within [TO BE COMPLETED: 2] business days** and offer a replacement or, failing that, a refund. **Don't send anything back before we reply**: photos are usually enough. If we need the piece, we pay the return postage.

Replacement or refund takes place within **30 days** of your request at the latest. The guarantee does not cover normal wear, or damage caused by a fall, an impact or repeated contact with water, perfume or chlorine.

**Care reminder: keep your CHARMED PARIS jewellery dry.** Take it off for showers, swimming, the sea, sport and sleep, and store it in its pouch or bag.

## 3. Late or lost parcel

Usual delivery time to France: **4 to 9 business days** after payment. Maximum commitment: [TO BE COMPLETED: 15] business days. If your tracking has not moved for several days, or shows "delivered" but nothing has arrived (check your mailbox, neighbours and pick-up point), write to us. We open an investigation with the carrier, then re-ship your order or refund you. Your parcel is our responsibility until you have it in hand.
