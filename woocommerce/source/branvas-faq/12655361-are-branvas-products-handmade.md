Partly. Branvas jewelry is made with a combination of hand work and precision manufacturing. Machines handle gold plating, stone cutting, and metal cutting so that every piece is consistent.

How to describe it in your store

Don't describe the jewelry as "handmade" or "handcrafted". In the United States, FTC guidance for jewelry reserves "handmade" for pieces that are shaped and finished entirely by hand, and other markets apply similar rules. Because plating and cutting are done by machine, Branvas pieces don't meet that standard, and a listing that claims it puts your store at risk.

Wording you can use instead:

- "Made with a combination of skilled hand work and precision manufacturing"

- "Quality-inspected before it ships"
