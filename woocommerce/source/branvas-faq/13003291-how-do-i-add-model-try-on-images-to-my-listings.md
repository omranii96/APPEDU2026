# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/13003291-how-do-i-add-model-try-on-images-to-my-listings (lu le 22/11/2026)

Two options, both available to all subscribed members:

- Public model image library: My Products → Actions → Edit listing → Additional product images. Select images, add them to "My product images," then Sync to store. New model images for a product are flagged with a small red dot on the My Products page.

- Custom AI model generation: My Products → Edit listing → the "+" button at the bottom of the image list. Generation takes a few minutes (you can navigate away); once satisfied, add the image to "My product images." Subject to your monthly AI credit quota (see What are AI credits and how do I get more?).

Models in both the library and custom generations are AI-generated.
