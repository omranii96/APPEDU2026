# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/14025924-will-branvas-ever-contact-my-customers-or-appear-in-their-orders (lu le 22/11/2026)

No. Branvas never emails, messages, or contacts your customers, and no Branvas logo or company information appears in the order, receipt, or package; it's completely blind shipping. All Branvas communication goes to you only. Tracking updates reach your customers under your brand through your store. Your customers are yours.
