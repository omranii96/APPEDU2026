There are no hidden fees: the costs are the subscription, product cost per order, $8.99 flat shipping, and the card processing fee (see What payment methods does Branvas accept, and what's the processing fee?). Full details at branvas.com/pricing.

Which plan should I start with?

Still exploring? Start Free and order samples. Ready to launch? Growth covers most new brands. Move to Scale once your catalog passes 75 products or the 3% discount outweighs the price difference. VIP is for high-volume brands that want priority processing, custom Thank You card, and an account manager.
