# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12893503-what-sales-channels-are-supported (lu le 22/11/2026)

You are free to sell Branvas products on any channel you choose. Branvas fulfills every order that reaches us, whether it syncs from a connected store or you place it manually.

Stores that connect directly

Shopify: fully integrated via our native app (apps.shopify.com/branvas). Products sync to your store, paid orders sync back for fulfillment, and setup takes about 10 minutes.

WooCommerce: native API integration, connected from the Connect Store tab.

Wix, Squarespace, Square, custom sites, or no store at all: see Can I use Branvas without Shopify?

Marketplaces (Etsy, eBay, Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop, and others)

Branvas places no restriction on selling through marketplaces. We do not connect to marketplaces directly, so a marketplace order reaches us in one of two ways:

- Through your connected store. If the marketplace is set up as a sales channel in your Shopify or WooCommerce store, its orders sync to Branvas like any other order from that store.

- As a manual order. Place the order yourself in Branvas with your customer's shipping address, then add the tracking number on the marketplace. See Can I use Branvas without Shopify? for the steps.

A few points to plan for:

- Marketplace rules are yours to meet. Each marketplace has its own requirements for listings, product categories, handling times, and returns. Compare its ship-by deadlines with our fulfillment and delivery times before you commit to them (see Where do orders ship from, and how long does delivery take?).

- Shipping addresses. TikTok Shop and Etsy do not pass the customer's address to Shopify, so you add it before fulfilling (see Fulfilling TikTok Shop or Etsy orders through Shopify).

- Warehouse and return addresses. When a marketplace asks for these, use your own business details (see What address should I use in my store and sales-channel settings?).

- Product identifiers. Branvas does not supply barcodes (see Do products have UPC, EAN, or GTIN barcodes?).

- Returns and claims. Branvas applies its own return policy to every order, whichever channel it came from (see What's your return policy?).

Brick-and-mortar and wholesale

For in-store stock, order products as sample orders shipped to your location. For larger quantities, see Can I order wholesale or in bulk?
