# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16976467-can-i-order-wholesale-or-in-bulk (lu le 22/11/2026)

Yes. Branvas is built for dropshipping one order at a time, but if you want to stock inventory, sell in person, or supply a retailer, we can quote a wholesale order for you. 

There is no minimum order quantity for wholesale; pricing depends on the products and the quantity.

To get a quote, message us through the in-app chat or email [email protected] and include the products you want (links or SKUs), the quantity of each, and your ship-to address. 

If you want branded packaging with the jewelry, say so, and tell us whether you want our standard branded packaging sets or fully custom packaging. Custom packaging is quoted as part of the same order.

We will reply with pricing and an estimated lead time.
