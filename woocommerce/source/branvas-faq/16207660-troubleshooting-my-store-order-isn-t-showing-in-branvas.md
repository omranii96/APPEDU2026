# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207660-troubleshooting-my-store-order-isn-t-showing-in-branvas (lu le 22/11/2026)

Work through three causes in order:

- Is the order paid? Only paid orders sync. An unpaid or pending Shopify order never appears, no matter how long you wait.

- Is the store connected? Connect Store should show green Connected. Reconnect if not.

- Was the product edited in Shopify? (Most common when 1 and 2 check out.) Editing or duplicating a product ID in Shopify breaks the link to the Branvas product, so its orders never arrive. Fix: republish the affected products from Branvas, and make all future edits inside Branvas. Product data flows one-way from Branvas to Shopify and overwrites Shopify-side changes. To confirm the connection is otherwise healthy, publish a fresh product and place a small test order on it.

Fulfill the stuck order manually so your customer isn't waiting: place it through Branvas as a sample order with your customer's shipping address (the packaging is identical to a normal dropshipped order), then add the tracking number to the Shopify order manually.
