# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12240928-how-does-branded-dropshipping-with-branvas-work (lu le 22/11/2026)

Dropshipping means you don't hold inventory: when a customer orders from your store, we ship the jewelry directly from our warehouse to them.

The process:

- Set up your brand. Create a free account at branvas.com and upload your logo.

- Choose your products. Browse the catalog and pick pieces that match your brand: necklaces, rings, earrings, bracelets, and brooches across our style collections.

- Publish to your store. Connect the Branvas Shopify app; products sync to your store and paid orders sync back to Branvas for fulfillment.

- We handle the rest. We quality-check each piece, pack it in your branded packaging, ship directly to your customer, and sync tracking back to your store automatically.

What makes it private label: every order ships under your branding alone. Presentation boxes carry your logo, the packaging is branded, and shipments show your brand name. Your customer only ever sees your brand, never Branvas.

The business model: your customer pays you directly through your store, so you keep control of your revenue from day one. You then pay Branvas only the product cost plus $8.99 flat shipping and packaging, after your customer has paid you. Set your own retail prices or use our Suggested Retail Price (60–70% margins, based on market research). Setup takes about 10 minutes.
