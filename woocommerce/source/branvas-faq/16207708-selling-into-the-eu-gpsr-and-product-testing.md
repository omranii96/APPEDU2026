# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207708-selling-into-the-eu-gpsr-and-product-testing (lu le 22/11/2026)

Selling Branvas products into the EU under your own brand makes you the economic operator / EU Responsible Person; Branvas is a U.S. company and doesn't hold that role for you. If you'd rather not hold it yourself, you can appoint a third-party EU Authorised Representative service directly.

Testing and REACH: Branvas doesn't hold blanket REACH or material-test reports for the whole catalog; testing for nickel, lead, and cadmium is done per SKU, with each report tied to that item. Need documentation? Email [email protected] with the specific SKUs; testing can be arranged at your cost. A paid inspection report typically costs $300-$800 per item, with a turnaround of around 15 business days; we confirm the exact price and timing in a quote before any testing starts. Don't market products as hypoallergenic, nickel-free, or lead-free in the EU unless you hold the reports.

VAT and IOSS: see VAT, IOSS, and sales tax: who handles what.
