Make a hero video

- Go to Marketing Studio and choose Hero Video.

- Pick a setting. Switch between Women's and Men's, then choose a scene.

- Pick a model from Recently used or the Model library.

- Click Generate. The finished video appears under History.

To use it in your store, download the video and upload it to the video or hero section of your Shopify theme.

Make storefront banners (Storefront refresh)

- Pick a style: The style sets the palette, type, and feel across every banner.

- Pick the aspect ratios you need. Each one becomes a separate banner sized to fit

- Pick a model.

- Pick the product to feature, from the pieces in My Products.

- Click Generate.

If a piece looks larger or smaller in a banner than it is in real life, remember these are styled marketing images. Keep the standard product images on your product pages for accurate size (see Why do pieces look different on models than in the product images?).

Campaign images

New product launch and Seasonal campaign work the same way: choose the moment or look, choose the products, and generate a matching set of images for your store and social channels.

Find and download what you made

Everything you generate is saved under History at the bottom of Marketing Studio. Filter by All, Images, or Videos, and use the download button on any item to save it.

Common questions

A generation failed. What now?

Failed items are marked Failed in History with the reason and a Retry button. If the message says the content was blocked by safety filters, retry with different copy or a different product. If a failed generation used an AI credit, contact us and we'll refund it.

Can I change the model's outfit or the scene details?

You choose the setting or style and the model. Clothing and scene details are generated to match that choice and can't be edited separately. If the result isn't right, generate again with a different setting, style, or model.

Can I add music or text to a video?

Not inside Marketing Studio. Download the video and add music or captions in your own editor or in the social app you post from.

Can I use these images and videos in ads?

Yes, on the same terms as other Branvas media. See Can I use Branvas media for my store and marketing?

Are these real people or UGC?

No. The models are AI-generated. Branvas does not supply videos from real customers or creators; for filmed content, see Does Branvas offer video content for my brand?

Where do I add model try-on images to a product listing?

That is done from My Products, not Marketing Studio. See How do I add model try-on images to my listings?

Where is the packaging mockup with my logo?

Under Logo Setup, in the package preview.

How do I build my whole store?

Choose Build my store in Marketing Studio to open SiteMuse. See Can you help me build my Shopify store?
