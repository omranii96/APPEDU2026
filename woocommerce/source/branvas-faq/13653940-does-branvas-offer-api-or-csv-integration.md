Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/13653940-does-branvas-offer-api-or-csv-integration (lu le 22/11/2026)

Yes, but by request only. API and bulk CSV integration are limited to VIP members, and VIP status alone does not grant access.

To request access, email [email protected] with your store URL, monthly order volume, and which option you need. We review each request and decide whether to enable it for your account. Approval is not guaranteed, even on the VIP plan.

The two options:

- API: for stores that cannot use our Shopify or WooCommerce integration, such as custom-built platforms.

- Bulk CSV: export orders from your platform and upload them to Branvas for fulfillment.

There is no public API documentation or self-serve signup. We share technical details with you after your request is approved.

Both options keep your private-label packaging. There are no setup, monthly, or per-transaction fees for approved accounts.

Most stores don't need either option. If you sell on Shopify or WooCommerce, connect your store directly; if you have no connected store, you can place orders manually. See Can I use Branvas without Shopify?
