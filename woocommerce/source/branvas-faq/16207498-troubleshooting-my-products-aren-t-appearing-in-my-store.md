# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207498-troubleshooting-my-products-aren-t-appearing-in-my-store (lu le 22/11/2026)

Three causes, in order of likelihood:

- Your free trial ended without a paid plan. All published products are automatically unpublished (they look like they vanished from your store). Your selections are saved: subscribe, then toggle Publish to Store back on in My Products.

- Your store isn't connected. Check Connect Store for green Connected; reconnect if needed, then toggle Publish to Store off and on.

- Neither? Email [email protected] with the product names and a screenshot from My Products.

Separate issue worth ruling out: if products exist in Shopify but nobody can see your store at all, it's Shopify-side storefront visibility (a password-protected or unpublished store), not syncing.
