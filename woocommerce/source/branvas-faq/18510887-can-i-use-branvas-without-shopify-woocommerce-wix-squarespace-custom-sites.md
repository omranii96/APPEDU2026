# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/18510887-can-i-use-branvas-without-shopify-woocommerce-wix-squarespace-custom-sites (lu le 22/11/2026)

Branvas connects to Shopify and WooCommerce. Other store platforms are not supported today. Here is where each one stands and what you can do instead.

Platform

Supported?

How it works

Shopify

Yes

Native Shopify app. Products sync to your store and paid orders sync back for fulfillment.

WooCommerce (WordPress)

Yes

API integration that you connect yourself. There is no plugin to install and no approval needed.

Wix

No

Not supported, and we have no timeline for it.

Squarespace, Square, and other site builders

No

Not supported.

Custom-coded or headless sites

No standard integration

API access is by request only, for VIP members, and is not guaranteed. See Does Branvas offer API or CSV integration?

Connecting a Shopify store

Go to Connect Store in your Branvas dashboard and install the Branvas Shopify app. Full steps: How do I connect my store and add products?

Connecting a WooCommerce store

- Log in at branvas.com.

- Open the Connect Store tab.

- Choose WooCommerce and follow the prompts to connect your store.

Once an order ships, the tracking number is sent back to your WooCommerce store automatically. See Where does tracking information appear in my WooCommerce store?

My site is on Wix, Squarespace, or another builder

You have two options:

- Move your store to Shopify or WooCommerce. If you are still early in your build, this is the fastest path to automatic product, order, and tracking sync.

- Place orders manually, as described below. This works on any platform but nothing syncs.

Ordering without a connected store

You can still use Branvas without a connected store by placing each order yourself as a sample order:

- Add the pieces to a sample order in Branvas.

- Enter your customer's address as the shipping address.

- Pay for the order. It ships directly to your customer in your branded packaging, the same as a regular dropshipped order (once your logo is uploaded and approved).

- Send the tracking number to your customer yourself.

Sample orders are available on every plan, including Free. This is also the way to handle in-person sales, gifts to influencers, and orders from a platform we don't connect to. Publishing products to a connected store requires a paid plan (see What are the membership plans and what do they include?).
