Christmas packaging

From Friday, November 20 through Thursday, December 24, every order you ship through Branvas goes out in our Christmas packaging, at no extra cost. Your logo stays on the box as usual.

What ships in the box

Scale and VIP: a more refined holiday presentation.

- Holiday wrapping paper

- Signature honeycomb wrap

- Decorative ribbon

- Christmas accents

- Complimentary Christmas card

Standard: festive and elegant, right for most gifts.

- Holiday wrapping paper

- Decorative ribbon

- Christmas accents

- Complimentary Christmas card

You can see the Christmas packaging with your own logo in Logo Setup -> Package preview.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. During the holiday window, every order from a subscribed member ships in Christmas packaging automatically. If you would rather keep your regular packaging, message us in chat and we will switch it off for your store.

Order-by dates for Christmas delivery

Carriers slow down in December and we expect two to three extra business days on every lane. For gifts to arrive before Christmas, your customers should order by:

- United States, United Kingdom and Australia: Tuesday, December 8

- Canada and Europe: Friday, December 4

- Everywhere else: Monday, November 30

These dates already include the holiday delay. Orders placed later may still arrive in time, but we cannot guarantee it. Add these dates to your announcement bar, product pages and checkout so your customers know.
