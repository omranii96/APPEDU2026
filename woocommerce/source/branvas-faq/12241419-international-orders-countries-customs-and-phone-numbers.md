# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241419-international-orders-countries-customs-and-phone-numbers (lu le 22/11/2026)

Branvas ships to the 101 countries and regions listed below. If you add a market and shipping rates don't appear, contact us to confirm coverage for that destination.

Asia & Middle East (32): Bahrain, Brunei, Cambodia, Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Cyprus, Georgia, Hong Kong SAR, Indonesia, Israel, Japan, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Macao SAR, Malaysia, Maldives, Myanmar (Burma), Oman, Pakistan, Philippines, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, United Arab Emirates, Vietnam

Europe (38): Åland Islands, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Moldova, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine, United Kingdom

Africa (14): Angola, Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Mauritius, Morocco, Nigeria, Réunion, Rwanda, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe

North & Central America and the Caribbean (6): Aruba, Canada, Costa Rica, Martinique, Mexico, United States.

South America (5): Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Peru

Oceania (6): Australia, Fiji, French Polynesia, New Zealand, Norfolk Island, Papua New Guinea

Puerto Rico is served as part of the United States: enter the address with United States as the country and PR as the state.

Not served: we do not currently deliver to India, Turkey, Russia and mainland China. Any destination that is not on the list above is also unavailable.

Customs and duty: nothing extra to pay. All shipments are DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): the $8.99 flat rate covers customs clearance and duty, so your customers never face surprise fees at delivery.

Enabling a new country: in Shopify admin go to Markets → Add market, select the country, save. The Branvas shipping profile picks it up automatically; manage pricing, currency, and duties per market in Shopify's Markets settings.

Phone numbers are required for delivery outside the U.S. and U.K. Carriers and customs use them for notifications, clearance, and delivery issues; a missing number can hold, fail, or return a shipment. In Shopify: Settings → Checkout → Shipping address phone number → Required → Save. Then place a test order to confirm the field shows as required.
