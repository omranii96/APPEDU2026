Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/14025944-how-often-are-new-products-added-and-what-does-coming-soon-mean (lu le 22/11/2026)

New products are added regularly, typically every month.

"Coming Soon" marks new products not yet released to all tiers. Scale and VIP members get early access and can publish them immediately; Growth members can publish them about one month after they first appear, but can order them as samples right away.

Tip: add products to your store at a steady pace rather than all at once. It keeps your store fresh for returning customers.
