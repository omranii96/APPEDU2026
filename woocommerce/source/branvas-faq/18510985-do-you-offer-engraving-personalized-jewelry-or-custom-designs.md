Short answer: catalog pieces ship exactly as shown and can't be engraved or personalized. If you want pieces made to your own design, that is done through VIP Custom Design, in a production run rather than one order at a time.

Engraving and personalization on catalog pieces

Not available. Every catalog piece is a ready-made, fixed design that we fulfill on demand, so we can't add names, initials, dates, messages, photos, or any other per-order customization. 

My logo on the jewelry itself

Not available on catalog pieces. Your branding goes on the packaging: your logo is stamped on the outer shipping box and the jewelry box (see What's included in branded packaging?).

My own designs (VIP Custom Design)

If you have your own design, sketch, or CAD file, or you want a piece nobody else can sell, we can manufacture it for you through VIP Custom Design. This is also the only route to engraved pieces: the engraving is part of the design and every piece in the run is made the same way.

- Plan: available to VIP members.

- Minimum order quantity: set by the manufacturer for each design, generally at least 500 pieces.

- Cost and lead time: quoted per design; both depend on the materials and complexity.

- How to start: email [email protected] with your design files or reference images, the materials you want, and the quantity you have in mind.

Because custom design means paying for a production run up front, we don't recommend it for a store that isn't live yet. Start with catalog pieces, and move to custom designs once your sales are steady.

What you can customize without a minimum

- Packaging: your logo on every order, on all paid plans.

- Your selection: no two Branvas stores carry the same lineup; you choose the pieces that fit your brand.

- Your listings: product names, descriptions, prices, and images are yours to set (see What can I customize in my product listings?).
