# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241086-can-i-use-my-own-packaging-or-create-custom-jewelry (lu le 22/11/2026)

Growth and Scale: standardized premium packaging customized with your logo (see What's included in branded packaging?).

VIP: fully custom packaging (size, material, and color), plus VIP Custom Design: completely custom jewelry manufactured to your specifications, with custom inserts and packaging. Both are subject to manufacturer MOQ requirements (generally at least 500 pieces per design), and design/production timelines vary with complexity. Contact the team to discuss requirements.

We recommend browsing the existing catalog and ordering samples first; if you need fully custom pieces, ask about VIP membership.
