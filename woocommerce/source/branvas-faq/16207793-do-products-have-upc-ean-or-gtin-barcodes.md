# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207793-do-products-have-upc-ean-or-gtin-barcodes (lu le 22/11/2026)

No. Branvas doesn't provide barcodes in the catalog, your listings, or the product data that syncs to your store. Channels that require an identifier (most commonly Google Ads/Shopping and Amazon Handmade) need you to register your own UPC/EAN and enter it on that platform. Check the channel's listing documentation before buying identifiers you may not need; if you're stuck on how to describe a Branvas product in a listing, email [email protected].
