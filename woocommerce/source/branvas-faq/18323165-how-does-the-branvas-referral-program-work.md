Invite a friend to Branvas and earn credit while they grow. They get $10 in credit when they sign up with your link, and you earn 30% of their subscription payments and 5% of their orders for 12 months. Your link is on the Referral page.

What do I get, and what does my friend get?

Your friend gets $10 in Branvas platform credit when they sign up through your link and confirm their email. They can use it on samples, orders, or their subscription.

You earn Branvas platform credit for 12 months from the day they sign up:

- 30% of every subscription payment they make

- 5% of every order they place

There's no limit on how many people you can refer.

How do I share my link?

On the Referral page you can copy your link, download it as an image with a QR code, or enter email addresses and we'll send the invitation for you. 

Who can I refer?

Anyone who has never had a Branvas account and whose store has never been connected to Branvas. You can't refer yourself, a second store you run, your business partners or staff, or anyone who shares your store, payment method, or address. Accounts connected in those ways don't earn credit for either side, and we may remove credit already earned.

If you invite someone who already has an account, we don't send the invitation, but they still appear in your list as Invited. We don't confirm whether an address belongs to a member.

How does a signup get linked to me?

Your friend must sign up within 90 days of clicking your link or the invitation we sent, and confirm their email. The click is what creates the link; there's no "referred by" field at signup, so a signup without a click can't be linked to you, even if the person meant to use your link. If they clicked more than one member's link, the last click before signup counts.

When does my credit arrive?

Each reward shows as Pending for 30 days after your friend pays, then moves to your available credit. The 30 days covers refunds: if your friend gets a refund or opens a payment dispute during that time, the reward is reduced or canceled.

What counts toward my reward?

For orders, the 5% is on the product amount after discounts. Shipping, taxes, and transaction fees don't count. For subscriptions, the 30% is on what your friend actually paid. Any part of a payment covered by credit or a promo code doesn't count, for orders or subscriptions.

What happens if my friend gets a refund or disputes a charge after I've been credited?

We add a Reversed entry to your credit history that takes back the matching amount, or a share of it for a partial refund. A payment dispute counts as a refund from the moment it's opened. If you've already spent the credit, your balance can go below $0 and is made up by your next rewards. We never charge your card for it.

What can I use my credit on?

Sample orders, customer orders, and your Branvas subscription. With "Apply credit to my plan" on, your available credit goes toward each renewal automatically, and toward your first payment when you subscribe or resubscribe.

Does my credit expire?

Not while you use Branvas. Credit expires only if your account has no sign-ins and no orders for 12 months in a row. We'll email you before that happens.

Can I cash out my credit?

No. Credit can only be used on Branvas and has no cash value. It isn't transferable, and it's forfeited if you delete your account.

What am I not allowed to do?

A few things will cost you the credit and can close your account:

- Referring yourself, a store you control, or trading referrals with another business for stores that already exist

- Running ads on "Branvas" or variations of it, on any ad platform

- Posting your link on coupon, deal, or cashback sites

- Sending your link as bulk or unsolicited email or messages, or using it in a way that breaks a community's rules

- Paying or offering anything to people for signing up

- Faking clicks, signups, or accounts, or placing your link so visitors get tagged without choosing to click

Where we think a referral breaks these rules we can hold, reduce, or reverse credit on both sides, and we may ask you to confirm that the referred business is independent of yours.

If you refer businesses as a service, for example as an agency or creator, the affiliate program is the right fit. You can hold a referral code or an affiliate code, not both.

What can I see about the people I refer?

For each referral you see a masked email (like a***@s***.com), when they joined, their status, and the credit you've earned from them. You don't see their name or their store. Your friend sees your name on the signup page and in their account. Statuses include:

- Invited: you sent an invitation and they haven't signed up yet

- Registered: signed up, not subscribed (their orders still earn you 5%)

- Subscribed: currently subscribed, within the 12-month window

- Canceled: subscribed before and has canceled; they go back to Subscribed if they resubscribe within the window

- Window closed: the 12 months have ended

- Not eligible: this signup didn't meet the program's requirements, so no credit is earned from it

Can the program change?

Yes. Branvas may change any part of the referral program, including reward amounts, eligibility, and how referrals are tracked, or pause or end it, at any time by updating the Referral page and this article. Changes apply to signups and payments after the change; credit you've already earned stays yours under the rules that applied when you earned it, subject to the refund and misuse rules above. Where there's a question about how the rules apply to a particular case, Branvas decides, acting reasonably and based on our records.

The full rules are in the referral section of our Terms of Service.
