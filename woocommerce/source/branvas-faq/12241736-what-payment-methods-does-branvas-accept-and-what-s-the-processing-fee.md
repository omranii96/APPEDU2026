# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241736-what-payment-methods-does-branvas-accept-and-what-s-the-processing-fee (lu le 22/11/2026)

All major credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Discover, and Diners. Our payment processor is PCI-compliant.

A processing fee of 2.9% + 30¢ applies to every transaction Branvas processes (it's the standard card fee charged by Stripe, our processor, not a Branvas fee). 

Currency: everything Branvas charges is in U.S. dollars (USD): product unit costs, the $8.99 flat shipping rate, and membership plans. Your card is charged in USD wherever you or your customers are located, and invoices are issued in USD only; they can't be issued in another currency. If your card is in a different currency, your bank converts the charge at its own rate.

The currency your own store displays is a separate setting. You can change the language and currency of your listings from the Product Listing tab (see What can I customize in my product listings?); that changes what your customers see, not what Branvas charges you.

Managing your payment methods

Branvas keeps two payment methods, and they are set in different places:

- Orders (sample and customer orders): go to Profile → Payment. Use Add payment method to add a card, Edit to update one, and Remove to delete one. The card marked Default is the one charged, including by Auto-Fulfillment.

- Subscription: on your Profile page, in the Subscription box, click Update payment method.

Changing the card in one place does not change the other. 

When you are charged

Orders are paid before they are fulfilled: you pay for a customer order when you click Fulfill, or automatically if Auto-Fulfillment is on. There is no cash on delivery, pay-later, or credit-terms option.

Branvas platform credit

Branvas platform credit is a balance on your Branvas account, shown on your Profile page. You receive it from approved claims that are resolved as a refund, and from the referral program (see How does the Branvas referral program work?).

- Credit is applied automatically to your sample and customer orders before your card is charged.

- To use credit toward your membership as well, open View history under Platform credit and turn on Apply credit to my plan.
