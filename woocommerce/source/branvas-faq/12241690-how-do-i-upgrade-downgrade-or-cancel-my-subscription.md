Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241690-how-do-i-upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel-my-subscription (lu le 22/11/2026)

Manage your subscription anytime from Dashboard → Profile → Subscription.

- Upgrades take effect immediately; you get the new features, limits, and AI credit allowance right away.

- Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle; you keep your current subscription until then. When the new (lower) publish limit takes effect, Branvas automatically unpublishes products newest-first until you're within the limit.

- Cancelling: you may cancel your membership subscription anytime. Your paid features stay active until the end of the current billing cycle; then your account moves to the Free plan. Once the subscription ends you can no longer fulfill orders through Branvas, and published products are unpublished (your selections stay saved, as in How does the 3-day free trial work, and what happens when it ends?).

- Pausing: there is no separate pause option, and you don't need one. Cancelling saves all your settings and product selections, and you can resume your subscription anytime and pick up where you left off.

- Uninstalling the Branvas Shopify app does not cancel your membership. Removing the Branvas app from your Shopify store leaves your Branvas subscription active, and you will continue to be billed. To stop billing, cancel the membership itself in Branvas under Profile → Subscription.

Subscription fees

When you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again.

- To avoid a renewal charge, cancel before your next payment date. The date is shown in the Subscription box on your Profile page.

- The free trial never turns into a paid plan by itself. It needs no card, and when it ends your account moves to the Free plan. You are charged only if you choose a plan and subscribe.

- Subscribed in the mobile app? That subscription is billed by the App Store or Google Play, so cancellations and refund requests go through your Apple or Google account (see Does Branvas have mobile apps?).

Think a charge is wrong? If you were charged after cancelling, charged twice, or charged an amount you don't recognize, contact us at [email protected] within 30 days of the charge, with the date and amount, before disputing it with your bank. 

We'll check the charge against your account and correct any error. Your invoices are under Profile → Invoices.
