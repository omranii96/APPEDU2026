# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/18511427-organizing-products-in-shopify-collections-tags-and-sort-order (lu le 22/11/2026)

How your products are grouped and ordered in your storefront is controlled in Shopify, not in Branvas. Branvas publishes each product to your store; you decide how to arrange them. The rule to remember: organize in Shopify, edit listing content in Branvas.

Collections

Collections are how Shopify groups products into pages such as "Necklaces" or "New arrivals". In your Shopify admin, go to Products → Collections and create a collection. You can add products by hand (a manual collection) or let Shopify add them automatically by a rule, for example every product with a certain tag (an automated collection). Adding a Branvas product to a collection does not affect syncing or order fulfillment.

Sort order

Open the collection in Shopify and change its Sort setting. Choose a preset such as best selling or newest, or choose Manually and drag the products into the order you want. Branvas has no setting that controls the order of products in your store.

Tags

You can add, edit, and remove tags in Branvas and have the tags synced to Shopify. Tags are useful for automated collections and storefront filters.

What not to do in Shopify

- Don't duplicate a Branvas product or recreate it by hand. The copy is not linked to Branvas.

- Don't edit titles, descriptions, or prices in Shopify. They are overwritten at the next sync.

- Don't add variants in Shopify. Manage variants in Branvas.

- Don't change the inventory quantity. The number shown is a placeholder, and Branvas sets it to 0 when a piece is out of stock (see Out of stock and restocking: how availability works).

For what you can change in your listings from Branvas, see What can I customize in my product listings?
