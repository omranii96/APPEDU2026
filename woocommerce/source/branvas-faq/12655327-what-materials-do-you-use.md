Gold plating is applied by eco-friendly electroplating and finished with an anti-tarnish e-coating. Stainless steel pieces are 304 stainless steel with PVD gold plating. All pieces are lead-free and nickel-safe.

Base metals and plating

- 925 sterling silver: genuine 925 silver. Every sterling silver piece is stamped with a 925 mark.

- Stainless steel: 304 stainless steel with PVD gold plating.

- Eco-brass: brass with electroplated gold and an anti-tarnish e-coating.

Gold karat: gold plating karat is stated in the Materials section of each product's detail page, so check it per piece instead of assuming one karat for a whole collection.

Gold-plated, not solid gold or vermeil: gold-tone pieces are gold-plated. We don't sell solid gold pieces, and we don't publish plating thickness, so don't describe a catalog piece as "gold vermeil" in your store. Vermeil is a regulated term with a minimum plating thickness in many markets.

Stones and pearls

- Moissanite: lab-created moissanite, not cubic zirconia. Stones of 0.1 ct and above ship with a GRA certificate (see Material certificates, test reports & compliance documents). The moissanite carat weight listed is the diamond-equivalent weight (DEW), not the actual physical stone weight. This is the standard convention across the moissanite industry.

- Pearls: genuine cultured freshwater pearls, not shell or imitation pearls. Describe them as genuine freshwater pearls; "natural pearl" means a non-cultured pearl and should not be used.

Where to find the specs for a piece

Each product's detail page in the catalog (after you log in) lists its specs in two places:

- Material section: base metal, plating and karat, and stone or pearl type and quality.

- Details: the dimensions and weight of the piece, and for necklaces and bracelets the chain length. Rings show either "adjustable" or the available sizes (see Ring sizing: adjustable rings, available sizes, and measurements).

Use these details as written in your own listings; they are the authoritative description of the piece.

If a detail you need isn't listed, message us with the product ID (the code starting with J, such as JN70XK) and the exact detail you need. We'll confirm it if we have it. 

We don't supply photos beyond those on the product page. If fit, scale, or feel matters for a piece, order a sample.

For tarnish, water, and wear, see Does the jewelry tarnish? Plating, testing, hypoallergenic, and daily wear.
