# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241434-where-do-orders-ship-from-and-how-long-does-delivery-take (lu le 22/11/2026)

We ship from multiple fulfillment centers in the United States and China. Each order is automatically routed to a fulfillment center based on your customer's location and product availability.

No EU warehouse: we do not have a fulfillment center in the EU, and orders to EU customers are not shipped from within the EU. Customs clearance and duties are still covered for every destination (see International orders: countries, customs, and phone numbers).

Routing is automatic: the fulfillment center is selected per order, so don't promise your customers that an order ships from a specific country or warehouse. If a sales channel asks you for a warehouse or ship-from address, see What address should I use in my store and sales-channel settings?

No express shipping: we offer a single shipping service and cannot expedite an order, so do not offer express, overnight, or guaranteed-date delivery at your checkout.

Carriers depend on the destination country: USPS for U.S. orders, DHL for most EU countries.

Below is the 90th-percentile delivery time for each destination, meaning 9 out of 10 orders arrive within it.

- United States: 3-8 business days

- Canada: 4-8 business days

- United Kingdom: 3-7 business days

- Australia: 3-8 business days

- Netherlands: 4-6 business days

- Spain: 4-9 business days

- Germany: 4-9 business days

- France: 4-9 business days

- Italy: 3-10 business days

- Ireland: 5-11 business days

- Rest of world: 4-12 business days

Business days exclude weekends. Public holidays in the destination country can add a day or two.
