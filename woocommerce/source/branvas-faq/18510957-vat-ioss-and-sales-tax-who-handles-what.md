Two different taxes are involved when you sell with Branvas: the import charges on the shipment, which we handle, and the tax on your retail sale, which you handle.

Item

Who handles it

Customs clearance, import duties, and import taxes on the shipment

Branvas. Covered by the $8.99 flat rate (DDP).

Sales tax on what Branvas charges you

None is charged.

VAT, GST, or sales tax on the price your customer pays you

You. Charge, report, and remit it under the rules of the markets you sell in.

VAT or sales tax registration and filing for your business

You. Branvas does not register or file on your behalf.

Import duties and taxes (DDP)

Every shipment to every destination is sent Delivered Duty Paid. The $8.99 flat rate covers customs clearance, duties, and applicable import taxes, so neither you nor your customer is asked to pay anything on delivery. 

IOSS

You do not need to give Branvas an IOSS number, and there is nowhere to enter one. Import VAT on EU shipments is settled as part of our DDP service.

Because the import side is handled for you, we can't attach your own IOSS number, or a marketplace's IOSS number, to an individual order, and we don't provide customs clearance documents for individual shipments.

Does Branvas charge me tax?

No. Branvas does not charge sales tax on fulfillment or sample orders. What you pay per order is the unit cost, the $8.99 flat rate, and the card processing fee (see Understanding pricing: unit cost, SRP, and your profit).

Is VAT included in the unit cost?

The unit cost is what you pay Branvas for the piece. It does not include the VAT, GST, or sales tax due on your own sale to your customer. When you set retail prices, add the tax your market requires on top of your costs, and configure tax collection in your store's tax settings.

What is the Tax ID field in my profile?

It is your own business tax number, for example a VAT, GST/HST, or EIN number. It is optional. If you add it under Profile → Account → Business Information, it is printed on your Branvas invoices. It is not an IOSS number and it does not change what you are charged. See Where do I find invoices, and how do I add my tax ID?

What Branvas can't tell you

Whether you must register for VAT or sales tax, which rate applies, and how to report your sales all depend on where your business is established and where your customers are. We don't give tax advice; confirm your setup with your accountant or tax authority.
