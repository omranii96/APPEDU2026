Moissanite stones of 0.1 ct and above ship with a GRA certificate; below that, no stone certificate is issued.

There is no self-serve download for other compliance documents. Email [email protected], naming the market you sell into and the document your marketplace or regulator asks for, if you need: Proposition 65 statements, REACH documentation, metal safety / heavy-metal test reports. Material testing is done per SKU (see Selling into the EU: GPSR and product testing for EU specifics). Paid inspection reports for specific products typically cost $300-$800 per item with a turnaround of around 15 business days; send us the product links for a quote.

On your own store you can state the metal, the plating, and that pieces are lead-free and nickel-safe. Don't describe a piece as certified, hallmarked, or independently tested unless you hold the document for that specific product.
