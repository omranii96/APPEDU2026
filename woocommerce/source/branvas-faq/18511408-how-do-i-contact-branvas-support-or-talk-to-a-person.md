# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/18511408-how-do-i-contact-branvas-support-or-talk-to-a-person (lu le 22/11/2026)

Support hours: our customer service team is online Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific Time. You can message us at any time, but expect a delay in our reply when you contact us outside those hours.

There are three ways to reach us.

Chat

Open the chat bubble in your Branvas dashboard or on branvas.com, or choose Need Help? in the dashboard sidebar. Our AI assistant answers first and handles most questions right away, at any hour. If your question needs a person, it hands the conversation to our Customer Success team, and a team member replies in the same chat.

You don't need to stay on the page. If you close the chat or step away, the reply is also sent to your email.

To get to a person faster, include the details up front: your order number, the product ID, and a screenshot or photo if something looks wrong.

Email

- [email protected] for general questions, orders, claims, and document requests.

- [email protected] for subscription and billing questions.

Write from the email address on your Branvas account so we can find your account quickly. Claims for damaged, defective, or incorrect items are answered within 3 business days (see What's your return policy?).

Video call

If you'd like a walkthrough of the platform or want to talk through your plans before you start, book a Google Meet with our team at branvas.com/contact
