# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/18511443-how-do-i-place-and-cancel-a-test-order (lu le 22/11/2026)

Placing a test order is a good way to check that your store, checkout, and Branvas connection work before you launch. A test order costs you nothing with Branvas as long as you don't pay for it in Branvas.

Before you test: turn Auto-Fulfillment off

Go to Customer Orders in your Branvas dashboard and make sure Auto-Fulfillment is off. With Auto-Fulfillment on, Branvas charges your saved card and sends the order to fulfillment as soon as it syncs, test order or not. Once an order is paid it enters processing and can't be cancelled (see Can I cancel an order? Can my customer?).

Place the test order

- In your store, place an order for a product you published from Branvas, using your store's test payment mode or a real payment you will refund yourself.

- Only paid store orders sync. Once the order shows as paid in your store, it appears in Branvas under Customer Orders with the status Payment needed.

- Seeing the order there confirms the connection works. Check that the product, quantity, and shipping address came through correctly.

An order in Payment needed is not charged, not produced, and not shipped. It stays that way until you pay for it in Branvas.

Cancel the test order

- In your Shopify admin, go to Orders and open the test order.

- Click More actions, then Cancel order.

Nothing more is needed in Branvas. As long as you don't pay for the order, it is never charged, processed, or shipped.

Common questions

I got a "Payment required" email about my test order. Do I need to do anything?

No. Branvas sends a reminder for any unpaid order. If it was a test, ignore the email or cancel the order in your store as described above.

My test order was paid and shipped because Auto-Fulfillment was on. What now?

Contact us right away with the order number. If the order has not entered production or packing we will try to stop it, but a paid order can't be cancelled once processing has started.

My test order doesn't appear in Branvas.

Check that the order is marked paid in your store, that Connect Store shows a green Connected status, and that the product was published from Branvas. See Troubleshooting: my store order isn't showing in Branvas.

I want to see the real product and packaging, not just the sync.

Place a sample order shipped to yourself. It arrives in the same branded packaging your customers receive (see Can I order samples before I commit?).
