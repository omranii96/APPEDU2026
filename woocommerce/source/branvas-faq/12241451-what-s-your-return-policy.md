# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12241451-what-s-your-return-policy (lu le 22/11/2026)

We replace or refund items that arrive damaged in transit, with a manufacturing defect, or incorrect (you received the wrong item), for sample orders and customer orders alike.

Because pieces can't be resold once shipped, we can't accept returns or offer refunds for:

- A change of mind

- Ordering the wrong size or style

- An item not matching personal expectations (color, look in person, etc.)

- General dissatisfaction that isn't quality-related

- An incorrect, incomplete, or undeliverable shipping address (see below)

File a claim within 10 days of delivery (the window starts when you or your customer receive the package, not the order date):

- Photograph the issue clearly: good lighting, the item in its packaging if possible, any packaging damage.

- Email [email protected] with the order number, a description, the photos, and whether it's a sample or customer order.

- We respond within 3 business days with your choice of a replacement at no cost or a refund issued as Branvas platform credit; approved resolutions ship or process within 1-3 business days.

If your customer receives a damaged item: have them send you a photo, forward it with the order number, and we'll ship the replacement directly to your customer. You keep them informed with the tracking we provide.

No exchanges for wrong size, style, or color preference; the customer places a new order, and any discount you offer them is your own customer-service decision.

Is there a warranty?

Branvas does not offer a separate warranty beyond the 10-day claim window above.

Claims cover damage in transit, manufacturing defects, and incorrect items; they do not cover normal wear, tarnish, or damage that happens after delivery. If you offer your customers a longer warranty, it is your own commitment and at your own cost. 

For how to care for the pieces, see Does the jewelry tarnish? Plating, testing, hypoallergenic, and daily wear.

Does the item have to be sent back?

Most claims are resolved from the photos, and we decide case by case whether a physical return is needed. When it is:

- We tell you once the claim is approved, and we provide a pre-paid shipping label. Neither you nor your customer pays return postage.

- The return address is issued by Branvas based on the customer's location. Items are never returned to your address, and Branvas's details never appear on the label.

- The item should be mailed back within 7 days of approval.

- There are no restocking or handling fees on approved claims.

Ask your customer not to send an item anywhere until we have replied to the claim. An item mailed back without a label from us can't be traced or credited.

Change-of-mind returns and the 14-day right of withdrawal (EU and UK sellers)

If the law in your market gives consumers a right to cancel an online purchase, for example the 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and UK, that obligation sits with you as the seller. Branvas's policy does not change: we don't accept, restock, or credit change-of-mind returns. In practice:

- You refund your customer under your own policy.

- The customer returns the item to an address you provide, not to Branvas.

- The returned piece is yours to keep or resell.

- Factor these returns into your pricing, and check with a local adviser which exceptions, if any, apply to your products.

Incorrect or undeliverable addresses

Orders ship to the address exactly as it appears on the order. A package is not covered by a claim if it is lost, returned, or delivered to the wrong place because of:

- An incorrect, incomplete, or outdated shipping address entered at checkout

- A missing or unreachable phone number where one is required

- An address the carrier cannot access (gated community, secure building, business closed at delivery time) with no alternative delivery instruction

- Delivery refused by the recipient, or a package left unclaimed at a post office, parcel locker, or pickup point until the carrier's holding period expires

In these cases:

- We can't replace or refund the order

- The customer places a new order if they still want the item; any discount you offer on it is your own decision

To avoid this:

- Check the address before you click Fulfill; leave Auto-Fulfillment off if you want that pause (see Can I change the shipping address after an order is placed?)

- Make the phone number field required in your Shopify checkout (Settings → Checkout → Shipping address phone number → Required)

- Publish the same rules to your customers using the shipping policy template

Templates for your store: the return policy template and shipping policy template are written to match the rules above, so you never promise your customers something we can't deliver.
