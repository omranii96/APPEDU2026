# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207469-how-do-i-search-and-filter-the-catalog (lu le 22/11/2026)

Browse from Catalog in your dashboard, or at branvas.com/catalog before logging in. The stone/material filter is limited (it exposes only a couple of stone types), and there's no filter yet for design themes such as occasion, motif, or religious designs. Instead, use the search function: it works for stone, style, theme, vibe, or product name.

Building a collection around a particular look? Place a sample order first; finish, stone, and weight in person tell you more than photos.
