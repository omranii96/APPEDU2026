Yes, if you act before the order enters production or packing (that's the cutoff, not "shipped vs not shipped").

- Before you pay (order in Payment needed): edit the address yourself before clicking Fulfill. Nothing has gone to the warehouse yet.

- After you pay: contact support in chat immediately with the order and the full corrected address exactly as it should appear on the label. We'll try, but changes after payment aren't guaranteed, and once production or packing starts they can't be made.

If address accuracy matters to you, leave Auto-Fulfillment off so every order pauses for your review while it's still free to fix.

My order is on hold: missing phone number or address problem

Branvas checks the shipping details on every order. If something is missing or the address can't be verified, the order can't be paid or fulfilled until it is fixed, and we email you with the subject "Action needed". The usual causes:

- No phone number on an order going outside the U.S. or U.K., or a number the local carrier can't use

- An incomplete address, for example a missing street number or apartment number

- A country that doesn't match the rest of the address

- An address the carrier reports as undeliverable

How to fix it and release the order:

- Confirm the correct details with your customer.

- In Branvas, go to Customer Orders and open the order.

- Edit the shipping address or phone number on the order details page and save.

- Click Fulfill and pay. Processing starts after payment.

Make the change in Branvas, not only in your store. When an address or phone number is added or changed in Shopify after the order has already synced, the update does not carry over to the existing order in Branvas.

If the order is already paid and we find a problem before shipping, we email you and wait for the corrected details. Reply with the full address exactly as it should appear on the label. If we can't deliver to the address and don't hear back from you, we cancel the order and return what you paid to your account as Branvas platform credit.

Prevent it: make the phone number field required at checkout (Shopify: Settings → Checkout → Shipping address phone number → Required → Save), and turn on address validation in your store's checkout. For what is and isn't covered when a delivery fails because of the address, see What's your return policy?
