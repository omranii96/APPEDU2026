# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/14025896-a-package-is-lost-or-shows-delivered-but-wasn-t-received (lu le 22/11/2026)

Delivered but not received (carrier misdelivery): check the delivery area (mailbox, porch, neighbors); contact the carrier with the tracking number, since they're best placed to investigate; and let us know so we can work with the carrier from our end.

Lost in transit: packages sometimes stall temporarily, so wait until tracking has shown no movement for 7 days past the estimated delivery window. Then contact support with your order number, no later than 30 days after the estimated delivery date. We'll investigate with the carrier and, if the package can't be located, arrange a replacement shipment or credit your account.
