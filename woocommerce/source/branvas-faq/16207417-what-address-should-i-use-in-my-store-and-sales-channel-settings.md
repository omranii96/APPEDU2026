# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207417-what-address-should-i-use-in-my-store-and-sales-channel-settings (lu le 22/11/2026)

Your own business details, always. Branvas is a private-label provider and does not issue sellers a warehouse address; our details must not appear in your store settings, on packaging, or on shipping/return labels.

- Use your own business or mailing address in address, origin, and return-address fields (they describe your business, not our facilities).

- Use your own branded support email in customer-facing contact fields, never [email protected], which is for you to reach us.

- Returns don't come to you: Branvas issues a return address based on the customer's location, with no Branvas information on it.

- TikTok Shop asks for a warehouse and return address at setup; the same rule applies, so use your own details. 

If a channel's validation rejects your details, email [email protected] with a screenshot.
