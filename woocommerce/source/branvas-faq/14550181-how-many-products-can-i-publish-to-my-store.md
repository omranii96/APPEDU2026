Growth 75, Scale 150, VIP 300; the Free plan doesn't include publishing. Your limit counts products live in your connected store at one time. Unpublish any product to free a slot, or upgrade instantly from your dashboard.

We cap publishing deliberately: members who publish a focused selection (roughly 25–100 pieces) consistently see more orders than those who flood their catalog. A tight lineup sharpens your brand identity, builds trust, and simplifies the buying decision. Start lean, learn what resonates, and expand with intention.
