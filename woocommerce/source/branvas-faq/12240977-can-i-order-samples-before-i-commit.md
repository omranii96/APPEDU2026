# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12240977-can-i-order-samples-before-i-commit (lu le 22/11/2026)

Yes, and we recommend it. Samples are the best way to check quality, materials, and packaging before you start selling. Sample orders are available on every plan, including Free.

First-time sample orders of $100 or more get $30 off with code NEW30. Sample orders ship in the same branded packaging as customer orders once your logo is approved (see What's included in branded packaging?), and follow the same damaged/defective-only return policy (see What's your return policy?).
