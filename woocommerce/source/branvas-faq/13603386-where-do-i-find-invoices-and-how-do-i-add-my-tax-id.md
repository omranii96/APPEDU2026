# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/13603386-where-do-i-find-invoices-and-how-do-i-add-my-tax-id (lu le 22/11/2026)

All invoices are under Profile → Invoices: monthly subscription, sample orders, and fulfillment charges. Add your tax ID under Profile → Account → Business Information and it will appear automatically on all invoices.
