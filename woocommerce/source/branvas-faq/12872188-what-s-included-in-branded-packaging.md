Common packaging questions

Can each piece in an order get its own jewelry box?

No. Each order ships with one jewelry box and one presentation set, whatever the number of pieces. A box per piece would make the parcel much larger. Every piece, including the one in the jewelry box, is sealed in its own reusable bag to protect the finish.

Can I buy extra packaging sets?

Yes, with a sample order. Extra packaging sets cost $3.75 per set. Place your sample order, then email [email protected] with the order number and the number of extra sets you want, and we'll email you an invoice for the extra packaging set. After the payment, we'll add them to that shipment.

If my customer places two orders, are they combined?

No. Orders are not merged: each order ships as its own parcel with its own packaging set, and the $8.99 flat rate applies to each one.

Can the jewelry box be a different color?

The standard jewelry box is metallic beige. A different color, size, or material is possible only through fully custom packaging on the VIP plan, which is subject to manufacturer minimums (see Can I use my own packaging or create custom jewelry?). For logo color, see Logo requirements, upload, and why the mockup looks different.

Can I add my own text to the packing slip?

No. The packing slip layout is fixed. Your own text is possible only on the Thank You card, on the VIP plan.

Can you include a gift message?

Gift messages are not a standard option, and there is no field for one on an order. 

The package is gift-ready as it ships: it arrives in the jewelry box with ribbon, and the packing slip shows no prices.

Where can I see the packaging with my logo?

In your dashboard under Logo Setup, in the Package Preview. To see and handle the real thing, place a sample order after your logo is approved.

Sustainability: most packaging is recyclable paper. Ribbons and PVC bags are designed for reuse.
