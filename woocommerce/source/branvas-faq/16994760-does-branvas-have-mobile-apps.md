# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16994760-does-branvas-have-mobile-apps (lu le 22/11/2026)

Yes. Branvas has a free mobile app for iPhone and Android. It uses the same login as branvas.com, so your catalog, store connection, and orders are the same in the app and on the web.

Download

iPhone: App Store 
Android: Google Play Store

What you can do in the app

- Browse the catalog and order samples at member pricing

- Add your logo to packaging and preview it before you order

- Connect your Shopify store, publish products, and track every order to delivery 

- Generate on-brand product images and short videos for your listings

Membership bought in the app

You can subscribe to a membership inside the app. Those subscriptions are billed and renewed through the App Store or Google Play, and you manage or cancel them in your Apple or Google account rather than in Branvas.com. If you subscribed on the web, keep managing your plan there (see How do I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription?).

Everything in the app is also available at branvas.com, so you can switch between phone and desktop at any time.
