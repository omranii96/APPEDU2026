# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/15032654-can-you-help-me-build-my-shopify-store (lu le 22/11/2026)

Yes. SiteMuse, Branvas's AI store builder for Shopify, generates a complete storefront (homepage, product pages, copy, and imagery) from your catalog selections in 5–10 minutes. No design or coding skills needed. 

Pick one aesthetic and up to three studio models; SiteMuse builds everything on native Shopify sections, so you can edit any element later in your Shopify admin. SiteMuse is available for Scale and VIP members. Generating a store uses 30 AI credits (see What are AI credits and how do I get more?).

To start: log in at branvas.com → Connect Store → install the SiteMuse Shopify app → launch SiteMuse from your Shopify admin.
