# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/13284375-how-do-i-connect-my-store-and-add-products (lu le 22/11/2026)

- Connect your store. Go to Connect Store and install Branvas Shopify app. A successful connection shows a green Connected status.

- Select products. In Catalog, click "Add to My Products" on pieces you want to sell.

- Publish. From My Products, toggle Publish to Store, or select multiple products and use Actions → Publish/Unpublish.

- Customize. Click "Edit Listing" to adjust pricing, change listing information (product title, description, materials, sizes, SEO), or swap product images. Click the sync button to update the edits to your store.

Make later edits (prices, titles, descriptions, sizes, images) inside Branvas, not in Shopify. Product data flows one-way from Branvas to Shopify and overwrites Shopify-side changes, and editing or duplicating a product in Shopify breaks the order-sync link (see Troubleshooting: my store order isn't showing in Branvas).
