# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/13048992-what-does-shipping-cost-and-how-should-i-charge-shipping-in-my-store (lu le 22/11/2026)

Branvas charges a flat $8.99 per order to all destinations, regardless of order size or location. It covers branding production, packaging, shipping, and customs (DDP, see International orders: countries, customs, and phone numbers), and is shown before you confirm each order.

Three shipping strategies you can choose for your store (set from Connect Store → click Change next to the shipping rate → choose → Save and publish to store):

- Free shipping: bake $8.99 into your product prices. Reduces cart abandonment; best when margins absorb it easily.

- Flat rate: charge customers a fixed fee (the default $8.99 breaks even).

- Conditional: paid shipping below a cart threshold, free above it. Grows average order value; a common rule of thumb is a threshold 20–30% above your current AOV.

Stores that also sell other products

Branvas products sit in their own Branvas shipping profile in Shopify. The rate you choose in Branvas (Connect Store → Change → Save and publish to store) applies to that profile only; the shipping rates for your other products stay as you set them in Shopify.

- Keep Branvas products in the Branvas shipping profile. If a product is moved to another profile, it can show as sold out and its shipping rate will no longer match your Branvas setting.

- Mixed carts: when a customer buys a Branvas product and another product in one order, Shopify adds the rates from both profiles together at checkout. If you offer free shipping on one and not the other, test a mixed cart so the total is what you intend.

- Fulfillment: Branvas fulfills only the Branvas items in a mixed order and syncs tracking for them. You or your other supplier ship the rest. Branvas charges you its $8.99 flat rate for its part of the order.

- "BRV Unsupported Regions": this zone in the Branvas shipping profile lists the destinations we do not deliver to. It has no rates on purpose, so customers in those countries cannot check out with a Branvas product. Do not add rates to it.

- Change the Branvas rate from Branvas, not Shopify. A rate edited directly in the Shopify profile can be overwritten the next time you save your shipping setting in Branvas.
