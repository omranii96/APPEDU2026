# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/16207801-variants-color-options-and-multi-item-orders (lu le 22/11/2026)

Multi-item orders ship as one parcel: everything is packed together regardless of item count.

A listing shows two colors but no color choice? The piece is most likely reversible by design (e.g. the Lucky Clover Beaded Bracelet is black on one side, white on the other): the two colors are the same product worn two ways.
