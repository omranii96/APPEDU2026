# Intercom

Source : https://faq.branvas.com/en/articles/12240857-do-i-need-inventory-or-a-minimum-order-quantity (lu le 22/11/2026)

No to both. You can start with zero inventory. We fulfill each order as it comes in from our ready-to-ship stock, and you only pay Branvas after your customer pays you.

There is no MOQ for regular dropshipping orders. MOQs apply only to specific optional programs: VIP custom jewelry design and VIP fully custom packaging (contact [email protected] to discuss those).
