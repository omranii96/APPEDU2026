## 6. Materials & care

**H1: Materials and care**

### Materials
- **Gold-plated brass.** Most of our sets are brass plated in real gold by electroplating, then sealed with an anti-tarnish coating. It isn't solid gold. The karat of the plating is shown on each product page when we have it.
- **White gold and rose gold plating.** Same process, in a silver or pink tone.
- **925 sterling silver.** Our 925 Silver sets are 92.5% pure silver. Every piece is stamped 925.
- **Cubic zirconia.** A lab-made stone cut like a diamond. Clear or coloured.
- **Moissanite.** A lab-grown stone, harder and brighter than cubic zirconia. Stones of 0.1 ct and above come with a GRA certificate. Weight is given in diamond equivalent.
- **Cultured freshwater pearls.** Genuine pearls, cultured. Ours are baroque, so each one is slightly different.
- **Resin, enamel, cord.** Listed on the product pages where they're used.

If you have an allergy or sensitive skin, write to us before you order and we'll send you the detailed composition of the set you're looking at.

### Care
- **Keep it dry.** Take your jewellery off before showering, bathing, swimming in a pool or the sea. Water, soap and chlorine wear down the plating.
- **Perfume and lotion first, jewellery last.** Let your skin dry before putting your set on.
- **Not for sport or sleep.** Sweat and friction tire the plating.
- **Store each set separately**, in its velvet pouch, so stones don't scratch other pieces.
- **Wipe after wearing** with the polishing cloth, no product needed.
- **Sterling silver darkens**, and that's normal: the polishing cloth brings the shine back. Keep it in its closed pouch.

Every parcel includes a card with this advice, a polishing cloth and a velvet pouch.

## 7. FAQ

**1. Can I wear my jewellery in the shower?**
No. Our sets need to stay dry. Take them off before showering, bathing or swimming: water, soap and chlorine wear down the plating.

**2. Is it gold?**
It's gold-plated, not solid gold. The brass is plated in real gold by electroplating, then sealed with an anti-tarnish coating. Our 925 Silver sets are sterling silver.

**3. Will it tarnish?**
The anti-tarnish coating protects the plating, but water, sweat and friction wear it down over time. Keep your set dry and store it in its pouch. Sterling silver darkens naturally; the polishing cloth brings it back.

**4. Is it nickel-free? Is it suitable for sensitive skin?**
We don't hold a test report for each design yet, so we don't make that claim. If you have a known allergy, write to us before ordering and we'll send you the detailed composition.

**5. Are the stones diamonds?**
No. Depending on the set, they're cubic zirconia or, in the 925 Silver line, lab-grown moissanite. Each product page names the stone.

**6. Does the moissanite come with a certificate?**
Yes, for stones of 0.1 ct and above: they come with a GRA certificate.

**7. Are the pearls real?**
The Liquid Art Pearl and Baroque Pearl Flow sets use cultured freshwater pearls. They're genuine cultured pearls, in baroque shapes.

**8. How do I choose a ring size?**
Most of our rings are adjustable: the band opens or closes slightly. When a ring comes in sizes, the product page asks you to pick one. Measurements are on each product page.

**9. How long will delivery take?**
9 parcels in 10 arrive in 4 to 9 business days in France and 3 to 7 in the UK, counted from your order. There's no express option.

**10. Where does my order ship from? Will I pay customs?**
Parcels leave from our partner's international warehouses and are delivered by DHL. Duties and import taxes are already paid: nothing to pay on arrival.

**11. How much is delivery?**

**12. Can I send it straight to the person I'm giving it to?**
Yes. Use their address for delivery and yours for billing. There are no prices in the parcel, and the set arrives in its drawer box tied with ribbon.

**13. Can I add a note or have it engraved?**

**14. If I order two sets, do I get two boxes?**
No: one order, one parcel, one box. The first set goes in the drawer box, the others in their sealed bags. For two gifts to two people, place two orders.

**15. Can I return my set if I don't like it?**
Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind. Return costs are on us, and we refund you within 14 days. If your set arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, send us photos and we'll replace or refund it.
